# Portfolio

Tejas Nirala's personal portfolio and resume: who he is, where he has worked, what he has built, and how to reach him.

## Identity

**Profile**:
The canonical description of Tejas: name, title, contact details, years of experience, headline, about text and skills. Every page and every share preview is derived from it.
_Avoid_: bio, user, me

**Headline**:
The large two-part statement that opens the About page.
_Avoid_: hero text, tagline

**Highlight**:
A short claim about one area of strength, shown as a card on the About page.
_Avoid_: feature, skill card

**Featured skill**:
One of the few skills chosen to appear on the social share image. Always also a listed skill.
_Avoid_: top skill

**Social link**:
A public profile on another site (LinkedIn, GitHub, TakeUForward).
_Avoid_: social, handle

## Resume

**Experience**:
One job: a role at a company over a period, with the work done there. The most recent experience is the current employer.
_Avoid_: job, position, employment

**Engagement**:
A body of work done within one experience, named after the product or client, with its achievements.
_Avoid_: experience project, sub-project

**Achievement**:
One resume bullet describing a result, led by a past-tense verb and backed by a metric where one exists.
_Avoid_: bullet, responsibility, task

**Education**:
A school attended, with the qualification earned and when.
_Avoid_: degree (a degree is one kind of qualification)

**Certification**:
A completed course or award from an institution, optionally with proof attached.
_Avoid_: certificate (that's the proof file), award

**Resume**:
The downloadable PDF that the site mirrors. When the two disagree, the resume wins.
_Avoid_: CV

## Work

**Featured project**:
An engagement chosen for the Projects page, told for a visitor rather than a recruiter: a short name, a qualitative summary, tags and a link to the live product. It never states metrics; those belong to achievements.
_Avoid_: project (ambiguous with engagement), portfolio item

**Short name**:
The compact name of an engagement, used where the full name won't fit.
_Avoid_: title, alias

## Presentation

**Page**:
One of the site's destinations: About, Resume, Projects, Contact.
_Avoid_: route, screen, tab

**Theme**:
The visitor's chosen appearance, dark or light. Dark unless they pick otherwise.
_Avoid_: mode, color scheme

**Entrance**:
The staggered arrival of a page's content when it opens.
_Avoid_: page transition, load animation

**Reveal**:
Content fading into place as it scrolls into view.
_Avoid_: scroll animation, fade-in
